No hate, I said,
and hoped the words
would teach the feeling.

No anger, I said,
while returning to the same scene
with the devotion of a bad historian.

No going back, I said,
and at least that sentence
knew what it meant.

I was not lying exactly.

I was announcing the person
I wanted to become
before I had finished
being the person who was hurt.

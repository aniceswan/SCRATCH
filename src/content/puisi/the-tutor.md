At first, I only wanted
for you to understand the question.

How to begin from what is given.
How to distinguish the unknown
from the impossible.
How to check whether the answer
survives a second reading.

I did not know
I was demonstrating a method
I would fail to use on myself.

When you smiled at a solved problem,
I called it progress.

For months,
I did not ask
progress toward what.

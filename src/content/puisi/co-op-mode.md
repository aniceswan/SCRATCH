The game did not remember
what I wanted from it.

It kept the scores,
the levels, the clumsy victories,
the small humiliations of losing
and asking for one more round.

It had no setting for
how a familiar voice
could make an ordinary evening
last longer than its hours.

Now the menu still offers
PLAY AGAIN.

I admire the innocence
of machines:
they cannot know
what counts as the same game.

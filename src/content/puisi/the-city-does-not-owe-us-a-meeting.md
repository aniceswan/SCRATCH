Someday we may walk
through the same crowded place
without knowing it.

Or perhaps the distance
will become permanent
without either of us deciding so.

I once wished
our roads would cross.
Then wished
they never would.

Now I am trying to wish
for less dramatic things:
that we arrive safely,
that we keep growing,
that no ordinary afternoon
must become a test
of whether we have healed.

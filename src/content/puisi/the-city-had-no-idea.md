Bandung went on being Bandung.
Motorbikes argued at intersections.
Students walked too fast for the weather.
Somewhere, another entrance exam
was becoming somebody's whole world.

The city did not know
I had reserved an invisible seat for you
in its future.

It did not know, either,
when I took the seat away.

To a city,
nothing happened.

To one person,
an entire itinerary ceased to exist.

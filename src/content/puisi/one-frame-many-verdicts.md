A photograph is excellent
at proving who appeared in a photograph.

It is less gifted
at explaining intention,
commitment,
timing,
or what two people believed
they owed each other.

I knew this,
even while my stomach tightened.

Knowledge can be accurate
and insufficient
at exactly the same time.

I had one frame.
I had a history of trust.

For a long night,
I could not make them
fit on the same page.

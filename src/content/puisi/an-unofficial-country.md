We never declared ourselves a country.
No border was drawn.
No document made the promise legal.

Still, I behaved
as though something had been entrusted to me:
a quiet territory,
a language we had started learning,
a flag no one else could see.

Maybe we disagreed
about its geography.

Maybe I called home
what you called a visit.

There is no court
for this particular difference.
Only two maps,
and the distance between them.

There was a time
a name on my phone
could change the temperature
of an otherwise ordinary evening.

The letters are still letters.
They have not confessed.
They have not apologized.
They have no idea
what they used to mean.

I think this is what time does best:
not erase the name,
but slowly remove
its power to interrupt
whatever else is happening.

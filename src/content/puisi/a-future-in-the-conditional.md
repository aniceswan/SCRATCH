If you got in,
I would show you the city.

If you had a free afternoon,
we could walk without a reason.

If the exams went well,
if the distance narrowed,
if the semester allowed,
if we were still us—

I lived for a while
inside that grammatical weather:
the conditional tense.

Nothing promised.
Everything possible.

A future composed almost entirely
of sentences
that never reached a period.

Anger can tell me
that something mattered.

It can make my hands restless,
my sentences reckless,
my certainty too large
for the evidence I possess.

It cannot tell me
everything you thought.

It cannot turn one image
into a complete biography.

Still, I will not shame it
for arriving.

It was the roughest language
my grief could find
when gentleness
stopped being enough.

You may come here someday.
I hope you do,
if this is still the place
your hard work is taking you.

The library will not ask
whom you once loved.
The lecture halls will not take sides.
The gates will open
for the person you have become,
not the story you left behind.

I used to imagine
being there when you arrived.

Now I imagine something harder:
that you arrive,
and I can be glad
without needing to witness it.

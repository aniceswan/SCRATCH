OBJECT:
A feeling once mistaken
for a certainty.

MATERIALS:
Late replies, entrance-exam questions,
a shared game,
a city not yet visited,
one unwelcome song.

CONDITION:
No longer in use.
Not entirely destroyed.

PROVENANCE:
Two people
who did not give the same name
to what was growing between them.

PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH.

The exhibit is more fragile
than its glass suggests.

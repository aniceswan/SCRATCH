First, invent a machine
that can return a sentence
to the mouth that spoke it.

Then find a way
to make a photograph unseen,
a suspicion unformed,
a trust untested.

Next, reconstruct
the person I was
before I learned
the cost of imagining us.

If all this succeeds,
do not call me.

The experiment has already proved
that even a perfect undo button
cannot tell me
whether I would choose
the same story again.

Who was that song for?

A harmless joke,
a borrowed romance,
someone else's declaration?

I do not know.

And that small admission
feels more difficult
than any accusation.

There are nights
when certainty is tempting
because it closes a sentence
pain has left open.

But I would rather write
“I felt betrayed”
than pretend I know
everything the photograph concealed.

I accept your apology.

I do not accept the idea
that accepting it
restores a previous version of us.

I wish you well.

I do not consent
to becoming a witness
to every good thing that follows.

I admit I still care.

I do not grant that feeling
the authority
to make decisions
on my behalf.

I have read the conditions.
For once,
I intend to keep my own.

There should be a different sound
for news that rearranges a person.

Not louder—
just honest about its weight.

A small click for a meme.
Two notes for a cancelled class.
A long, low bell
for the instant a name
stops meaning what it meant yesterday.

But phones are terribly democratic.

Everything arrives
with almost the same light.

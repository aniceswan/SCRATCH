You said you were sorry.

Past tense:
what happened.

Present tense:
how I am living with it.

Future tense:
what neither of us can promise.

An apology is a sentence.
It can be necessary,
earnest,
well meant.

But it cannot conjugate
my body back
into the morning
before I needed it.

Ten years from now
I may read this
and disagree with the boy
who wrote it.

I may find him too severe,
too hopeful,
too eager for a reason,
too frightened of being wrong.

I hope I am gentle with him.

He was young enough
to believe a shared future
could begin in a lesson,
and old enough
to know that sometimes
walking away
is the last answer
he can honestly give.

The museum will remain,
though the exhibits may change.

And perhaps, one day,
I will enter it
not looking for you,
not looking for proof,

only looking at who I was
when all of this
still mattered so much.
